## III. Branching Mechanism

At node v, quantum decoherence drives the Dimension tree to branch:

Node v
├── Branch 1 (probability 40%)
│ └── ... (continues evolving)
├── Branch 2 (probability 35%)
│ └── ... (continues evolving)
└── Branch 3 (probability 25%)
└── ... (continues evolving)
Branch probabilities are biased by the hidden variable λ:

$$P(\text{branch}_i) = |\langle i|\Psi(v)\rangle|^2 + \epsilon_i(\lambda)$$

Decoherence condition:

$$\tau_{\text{decoherence}} \gg \tau_{\text{coupling}}$$
